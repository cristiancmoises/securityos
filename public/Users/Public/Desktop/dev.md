# Cristian Cezar Moisés

**Security researcher · Ethical hacker · Sole maintainer & developer of SecurityOS & Vaptvupt**

I build **SecurityOS** — a privacy-first, **Tor-only web operating system** for
security work — and **Vaptvupt**, its built-in post-quantum encryption tool
(producing `.zupt` files). SecurityOS is an **independent, one-person project**:
no team, no company, no investors. I develop it **alone**, in the open.

> "Security and privacy are not features — they are rights. I build SecurityOS to
> make them usable for everyone."

## 🎯 Why I build this

I develop SecurityOS **to improve people's privacy around the world** — to help
anyone be **safer and more secure online** — and for **my own personal use**. It
is a tool for defenders, researchers, journalists, students and the
privacy-conscious. It is **not** built for, and I do **not** condone, any illegal
or unauthorized activity. See **`terms.md`** on this desktop.
